Performance evidence

Measure first. Claim second.

VIB now has a working VIR lowering, verification and execution subset, plus opt-in `.vye` packaging and caching. Native machine-code generation is still not implemented, so this page publishes the measurement plan rather than unsupported native-performance results or projected speedups.

Evidence protocol

  • Record the source commit, compiler revision, OS, hardware, target and all flags.
  • Publish exact commands, inputs, repetitions, warm-up policy, median and spread.
  • Separate compilation time from runtime and startup costs.
  • Compare equivalent semantics and observable output, not merely similarly named programs.
  • Preserve raw results and disclose missing, flaky or platform-specific measurements.
  • Keep predictions separate from measured results and document what would disprove each prediction.
