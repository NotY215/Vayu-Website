WHY YOUR SUPPORT MATTERS

Programming takes more than typing code.

Independent development is a long-term effort. Planning architecture, writing and reviewing code, reproducing bugs, testing builds, documenting changes and maintaining websites all take hours of concentration. My computer also uses electricity during development, builds and testing, and hardware and tools need ongoing care.

I work on these projects because I want to create useful software and share the results. Donations can help cover practical costs, give me more room to spend time on development, and provide encouragement to keep improving the projects rather than having to put that time elsewhere. There is no obligation, and every amount is appreciated.
